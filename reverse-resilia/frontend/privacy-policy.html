PRIVACY POLICY

ReverseResilia is an offline-first academic hackathon demonstration application built. We respect operational privacy and maintain zero third-party telemetry, tracking, or cloud data transmission.

1. LOCAL DATA PROCESSING

All network graphs, facility coordinates, customer orders, and optimization calculations are processed strictly on localhost. No supply chain data is transmitted to external servers or third-party cloud services.

2. NO PAID APIS OR COOKIES

ReverseResilia uses zero paid tracking services, zero third-party advertising scripts, and zero commercial solver telemetry. The application operates 100% offline without external dependency.

3. CONTACT & ATTRIBUTION

Built by Runtime Rebels: Runtime Rebels at Dr. Ambedkar Institute of Technology, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.
