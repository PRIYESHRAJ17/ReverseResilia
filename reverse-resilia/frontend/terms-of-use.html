TERMS OF USE

Welcome to ReverseResilia. These Terms govern your evaluation of the ReverseResilia goal-driven supply chain recovery platform, built by Runtime Rebels.

1. ACADEMIC & DEMONSTRATION PURPOSE

ReverseResilia is an interactive decision-support system designed to demonstrate mathematical recovery under facility outages. All algorithms and models are free and open-source.

2. REPRODUCIBILITY & OPEN CODE

All optimization code, datasets, and verifier scripts are provided for academic evaluation, peer inspection, and reproducible benchmarks.

3. SOURCED GEOGRAPHY & SIMULATION

Network road coordinates are sourced from authentic Indian PIN directories and OpenStreetMap extracts. Facility inventories, order deadlines, and cost models are simulated under transparent hackathon constraints.

4. ACADEMIC AND HACKATHON PURPOSE

ReverseResilia was created by Runtime Rebels Open Innovation track at Dr. Ambedkar Institute of Technology, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.
